About Me & Experience

Healthcare Background

Before pivoting into technology, I built a 13-year career in healthcare as a Licensed Practical Nurse and a Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) Specialist. During my tenure at institutions like the University of Illinois Chicago and Mind Body Co-op, I managed sensitive patient data via Epic and AdvancedMD databases, maintained 100% HIPAA compliance, and achieved 99% hardware uptime through proactive technical troubleshooting of medical equipment.

Pivoting to Cybersecurity

My passion for information security, AI technology, and cloud architecture drove me to transition into the tech sector. I am leveraging my extensive background in compliance and regulatory documentation to build a strong foundation in cybersecurity defense, automation, and web development. In addition, I actively seek opportunities to collaborate on real-world projects and contribute to open-source initiatives.
